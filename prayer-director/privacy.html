Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Overview

Prayer Director is designed to work on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not operate a backend that collects your prayer or location history.

Location

The app uses your device’s location (when you grant permission) only to calculate the Qibla direction relative to the Kaaba. Location is processed on-device for this purpose and is not uploaded to our servers.

Motion & compass sensors

Compass and motion sensors are used on-device to orient the dial. This sensor data stays on your device.

Analytics & advertising

Prayer Director does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics trackers that identify you across apps.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent via the support form.
