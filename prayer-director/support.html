Common tips

  • Allow Location when prompted so the app can compute the Qibla bearing from where you are.
  • If the compass seems off, move away from magnets, metal cases, and chargers, then recalibrate by moving your phone in a figure‑eight.
  • For best results when standing, keep the phone relatively level; tilt guidance appears when the device is steeply angled.
  • Near Masjid al-Haram, bearing can be unstable — the app may show you are at the Sacred Mosque instead of a direction.
